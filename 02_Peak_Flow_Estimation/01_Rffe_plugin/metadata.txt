about=Runs the RFFE 2021 model (rffe-2021.wmawater.com.au) without leaving QGIS. Pick the catchment outlet and centroid directly on the map canvas in any project CRS, enter the catchment area, and get design flood estimates for the 50%, 20%, 10%, 5%, 2% and 1% AEPs with 90% confidence limits. Results are written to a chosen output folder as CSV and JSON, and loaded into the project as layers: the fitted catchment polygon, outlet/centroid points carrying the recommended model's flows, and the gauged stations the regional models were fitted from (with record lengths, distances and at-site flood frequency curves) so you can judge how well constrained the estimate is. Requires an internet connection; no login or API key. Note the RFFE website's own dataset zip download is currently broken server-side, so catchment/stream shapefiles and map images are unavailable - the station data is recovered by other means.
